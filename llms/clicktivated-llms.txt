# Clicktivated

> Clicktivated is a video technology platform that turns existing video content into interactive, shoppable experiences without re-shooting or re-editing. Its "Activate, Distribute, Optimize" workflow makes any video clickable the same day, deploys it programmatically across DSPs, SSPs, publishers and direct site embeds, and captures first-party intent signals — item-level and place-level — to improve targeting, measure lift and drive performance. Founded as Clicktivated Video, Inc. and backed by Techstars, with offices in Detroit, New York, Dubai and Sydney.

## Product hosts

Clicktivated's product runs on its own second domain, clicktivatedstudio.com. This is
first-party: clicktivated.com's homepage embeds the player from it directly, and the
player bundle served there identifies itself as "prod-cvi-player" (Clicktivated Video,
Inc.).

- Player (public embed): https://player.clicktivatedstudio.com/watch?v=<videoHash>  [200]
- Customer dashboard: https://dashboard.clicktivatedstudio.com/  [403, Cloudflare challenge + login]
- API host: https://api.clicktivatedstudio.com  [most paths 403, Cloudflare challenge]
- Asset CDN: https://cdn.clicktivatedstudio.com

## Integration surface

The only integration a customer performs is an iframe embed:

    <iframe src="https://player.clicktivatedstudio.com/watch?v=707975ec4b"
            frameborder="0" allowfullscreen scrolling="no"
            width="100%" height="100%"></iframe>

Query parameters observed:
- `v` (required) — opaque 10-hex-character video hash issued by Clicktivated.
- `type=vast` (or a `vast` parameter, or a `/vast/` path segment) — switches the player
  into IAB VAST ad-serving mode with a progressive MP4 source and the VPAID 2.0 bridge.

There is no versioned build and no package: the embed is an unpinned iframe against a
rolling deployment, so an integrator cannot pin a player version.

## Standards actually implemented

Read from Clicktivated's own shipped player bundle:
- IAB VAST — VAST playback mode selection.
- IAB VPAID 2.0 — full creative message bridge (clickThru, expandNonlinear,
  collapseNonlinear, requestPlay/Pause/Stop/Skip/Resize, requestFullScreen,
  requestExitFullScreen, requestChangeVolume, requestChangeAdDuration,
  requestNavigation, getMediaState, reportTracking, log, fatalError).
- IAB viewability states — VIEWABLE_IMPRESSION, NOT_VIEWABLE_IMPRESSION,
  VIEW_UNDETERMINED_IMPRESSION.

## Player interaction events

First-party client telemetry sent to https://api.clicktivatedstudio.com/analytics as a
sendBeacon GET. This is Clicktivated's own ingest, NOT a customer-facing webhook or
event API; there is no subscription and no published schema.

VIDEO_LOAD, FIRST_PLAY, VIDEO_COMPLETED, SHAPE_CLICK, NO_SHAPE_CLICK, BOOKMARK_CLICK,
END_CAROUSEL_BOOKMARK_CLICK, CTA_CLICK, SHOW_BOOKMARKS_CLICK, HIDE_BOOKMARKS_CLICK,
FULLSCREEN_CLICK, EXIT_FULLSCREEN_CLICK, ERROR.
